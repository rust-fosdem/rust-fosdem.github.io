### Overview

Welcome to the 8th edition of the Rust devroom,
organized as part of [FOSDEM 2027](https://fosdem.org/2027/). FOSDEM is an annual
conference about free and open source software, attended by over 5000
developers and open-source enthusiasts from all over the world.

This devroom is a conference-within-a-conference that gives us the
opportunity to come together and chat, hack, and share stories about Rust usage
in FLOSS ecosystems.

FOSDEM 2027 will take place on the weekend of 30 and 31 January 2027, at
[ULB (Campus Solbosch)](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/13699100), in Brussels, Belgium.

The Rust devroom is scheduled on Sunday 31 January 2027, from 09:00 CET to 17:00 CET.

Join us to enjoy a full day of talks, demos and interesting discussions on Rust.

Sounds interesting? Submit your talk proposal below and see you in Brussels!
